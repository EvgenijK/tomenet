## Зависимости и граница

в C. Random/DNA choice semantics, встречающиеся в полном baseline outcome,
проверяются без принятия `birth.restore-dna` или `birth.save-dna`.

Guide capability принадлежит G. Если canonical birth obligation вызывает Guide
entry, в B показывается точная временная native placeholder и возвращается тот же
caller без изменения selection/draft; это проверка birth owner, не Guide coverage.

### Unsupported character-creation setup

После удаления служебного contact-бита `0x02` поддерживаются только
`creation_flags == 0` (traditional stat order) и `creation_flags == 1`
(player-defined stats). Любое нормализованное значение `creation_flags > 1`
является неподдерживаемым режимом создания персонажа. Попытка ordinary или
dedicated/exclusive creation немедленно показывает точный текст
`Unsupported character creation mode` и оставляет игрока на character selection.
Она не открывает name/birth interaction, не мутирует birth draft и не отправляет
name, birth, partial или completed-character packet.

Это явно принятое отличие SV от behavior baseline для неизвестных и будущих
режимов: SV fail-fast, а не предполагает семантику неизвестного server setup.

Проверка выполняется на production endpoint seam для ordinary и
dedicated/exclusive creation в обоих compile orders и подтверждает точный текст,
неизменный character-selection state и отсутствие draft mutation и packet send.

- D-d037649996d38bb2: resolved; integrated repair cycles: 1/3; The encoded IDDC and PvP limits are subtracted from ordinary capacity even when their feature flags are absent. For a normal 12/2/1 server with neither dedicated feature, total_limit is 12 but reserved is 3, so baseline_ordinary_limit becomes 9; with only IDDC it becomes 11. Legacy keeps the ordinary limit at 12 and adds only supported dedicated capacity. Accounts with otherwise legal ordinary slots are therefore prevented from entering the SV-B-023 prerequisite flow. Existing ordinary-create tests always advertise both dedicated flags and do not expose this case.
- D-1563ec778f0a5c2f: resolved; integrated repair cycles: 1/3; The mandatory unavailable-body-step path cannot occur: prepare_bodies unconditionally clears body_skipped and always installs Normal body. Consequently, class/stats backtracking branches that consult body_skipped can never bypass an unavailable body owner, and current tests only cover an unavailable Fruit Bat choice while the body step itself remains active.
- D-3dd8c1894797fcf5: resolved; integrated repair cycles: 1/3; The unavailable-body repair treats pre-4.4.1a servers as having no body choice and skips the owner, but it does not preserve the historical Fruit Bat outcome that those protocol versions exposed as a mode choice. `prepare_modes` has no Fruit Bat alternative, and body DNA/random input is never applied after the skip. The focused unavailable-body scenario backtracks and quits before mode, so exact-HEAD sv-core does not expose the loss.
